Trust Replay — see how you move onchain

Turn a user's onchain history into a personalized, shareable identity that creates a low-friction acquisition funnel into Trust Wallet.

1

The opportunity

  • A history with no mirror. Crypto users already carry a rich onchain history, but most wallets never turn that data into a personal identity.
  • Proven shareability. "Wrapped" mechanics have proven highly shareable in consumer products, people want to see and post their own story.
  • Lower the funnel. Instead of asking a new user to download or connect a wallet first, let them enter an address and get something personalized immediately.
2

Proof / market signals

The "Wrapped" mechanic already has proven consumer behavior.

  • Spotify Wrapped150M+ unique engaged users across 111+ markets in 2022, and 227M monthly active users engaged with Wrapped in 2023. Demonstrating the power of personalized, data-driven experiences as a social and engagement mechanic.
  • OKX RewindBrought the year-in-review format into Web3 personalized trading journeys, milestones, network activity, DeFi/meme activity and shareable cards.
  • Binance Year in ReviewPersonalized year-in-review experiences surfacing users' trading activity, milestones and personalized profiles.
  • Soken WrappedA more direct Web3 precedent. Users enter a public wallet address without connecting a wallet or signing a transaction, and get a personalized onchain breakdown: activity, chain distribution, behavioral patterns and a wallet personality.
The opportunity: the mechanic is already familiar to consumers, while Web3 is beginning to apply it specifically to onchain behavior. Trust Wallet can take this one step further by turning the result into an acquisition funnel Allowing users to discover their onchain identity first, then introducing Trust Wallet as the next step.

Trust Replay Growth concept — Wayne Choo

Trust Sidequest — complete quests. Explore ecosystems. Unlock rewards.

Create a recurring seasonal campaign that turns Trust Wallet's DeFi Trading focus, ecosystem partnerships and new product launches into repeatable acquisition, engagement and retention.

Example: Season 01 — DeFi · Season 02 — bStocks · Season 03 — AI · Season 04 — Memecoins

1

The opportunity

  • Beyond holding crypto. Trust Wallet is expanding its focus toward DeFi Trading and new onchain experiences.
  • Repeatable beats one-off. Individual product launches drive a single spike, a repeatable campaign framework compounds instead, giving users a reason to explore, participate and return.
  • A partner platform. A seasonal format creates a natural stage for ecosystem partners, KOLs and communities to participate.
2

Proof it works — OpenSea Voyages

Seasonal quest campaigns aren't theoretical. OpenSea's Voyages system already showed the mechanic drives real activity.

  • +44% user activityOpenSea's OS2 launch, anchored by the Voyages quest system, drove a 44% increase in user activity.
  • Address activity surgedWallet activity moved in tandem with the update as users completed quests to earn XP toward the SEA token airdrop.
  • Market share reclaimedOpenSea regained ground from rivals like Blur, whose token incentives had previously pulled pro traders away.
My dashboard: I built a Dune dashboard tracking Voyages activity end-to-end to validate this pattern before proposing it for Trust Wallet.

The reward system

Season XP → Levels → Rewards.

  • Earned through curated missions

  • Builds a longer-term user profile

  • Trust Wallet benefits
  • Digital collectibles / badges
  • Campaign access
Short-term competition → Season XP vs. Long-term identity → Persistent achievements
